# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-23)
The first complete release: the whole career from the night shift to Vice President, Global Safety & Security.

### The career
- Ten career levels, 34 ladder cases (M001-M034), four career crises (C001 power outage, C002 bomb threat call, C003 fire on a guest floor, C004 hurricane at the Cliffside Resort) and the capstone X001, One Night, Three Hotels.
- Every case is played on foot in one walkable, multi-floor hotel (B1 LP office and CCTV room, ground floor lobby, kitchen and front desk, floor 1 ballroom, floor 4 executive offices and boardroom, floor 14 guest rooms, Stair A), with the people, tools and evidence in the world.
- A hidden seven-part rubric scored only when the report is filed; a debrief that explains best practice and why; 47 Field Manual cards; a 47-term glossary.
- Consequences that come back: weak decisions schedule seeded later events (claims, grievances, findings, insurer letters, press) that name the decision behind them.
- Promotion on evidence of competence and passed cases, never XP or relationships.
- Eleven endings chosen by how you worked, a performance review and a certificate you can save.

### Modes
- Survival night shift, daily cases (the same three for everyone on a date) and five classroom sets, scored like the career, saved separately, with a local leaderboard.

### Options and accessibility
- Text scale, reduced motion, colour-safe palette, invert Y, mouse and gamepad sensitivity, frame cap, vsync, upscaling, hints by difficulty.
- Recorded voices for every fixed and spoken line (380 lines, Kokoro-82M, offline).

### Notes
- Offline and single-player. All businesses and people are fictional. Training-style fiction, not legal advice.
