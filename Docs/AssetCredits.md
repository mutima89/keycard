## Logo and brand check
Every downloaded model and texture is inspected for real logos or brand text before use (contact sheet of all diffuse maps, then zoomed crops). Results are recorded here per asset after the first fetch on the PC. Any mark found is painted out through `EDITS` in `Scripts/FetchAssets.py` or the asset is excluded.
